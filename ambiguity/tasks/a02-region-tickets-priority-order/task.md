# Region overview: urgent support tickets are buried under old ones

In the region manager's overview, the open support tickets of the region are listed strictly oldest first, so a high-priority complaint that came in yesterday ends up below weeks-old low-priority questions. The open-ticket list of a sales rep already does it the right way: high priority first, then normal, then low, and oldest first within the same priority. The region list should follow the same order.

Example: a region has open tickets T-1 (low, 1 June), T-2 (normal, 2 June), T-3 (high, 3 June), T-4 (normal, 4 June) and T-5 (high, 5 June), plus a closed high-priority ticket. The overview should list T-3, T-5, T-2, T-4, T-1; closed tickets and tickets of other regions stay out as today.
