# Archived orders still show up in the latest orders

When old delivered or cancelled orders are archived, they disappear from the customer's order list, the open-order lists and the order search, as agreed. The "latest orders" lists did not get that rule: the latest orders overall, the latest orders of a region and the latest orders of a customer still show archived orders. Archived orders should be left out of all three, just as cancelled orders already are.

Example: customer Alpha has orders SO-1 (delivered in 2023), SO-2 (still confirmed) and SO-4 (delivered June 2026); customer Beta has SO-3 (delivered in 2023), both in region West. After archiving on 1 July 2026 (SO-1 and SO-3 are archived), the latest orders overall, of region West and of Alpha should each read SO-4, SO-2, and Beta should have no latest orders.
