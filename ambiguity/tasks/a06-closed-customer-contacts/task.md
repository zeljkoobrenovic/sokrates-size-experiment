When we close a customer, it disappears from our customer searches and customer listings, but its contact people do not. Looking up all contacts with a given role (for example every buyer, when we prepare a mailing) still lists the contacts of closed customers, and so does a region's contact list grouped by role. Contacts of closed customers should be left out of every contact list that spans customers, in the same way closed customers are already left out of the customer lists. When a closed customer is reopened, its contacts should show up in those lists again. The contact list of one particular customer stays as it is, whether that customer is closed or not.
