Finance wants the same quarterly view for payments that we already have for customers, orders, invoices, shipments, returns and purchase orders. For a given year, report for each of the four quarters, in order, the number of payments received in that quarter, in total and per region of the paying customer. Quarters and regions without payments are shown with zero, so the report always has four quarters and every region. Payments on invoices that have been cancelled are not counted. For example, two payments by a North customer in January and March and one by a South customer in February give quarter 1 a total of 3 (North 2, South 1, East 0, West 0), and a year without payments gives four quarters of zeros.
