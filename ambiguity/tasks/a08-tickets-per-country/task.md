Support wants to see where tickets come from, the way we already list orders, invoices, shipments, returns, purchase orders and customers per country. Give the support tickets of the customers of one country opened between two dates (both inclusive), whatever their status (open, in progress or closed), oldest first and, for the same opening date, in the order they were created. Also give, for two dates, the number of tickets opened in that period per customer country, highest count first and alphabetically by country for equal counts; countries without tickets in the period are left out. For example, four tickets of UK customers, two of US customers and one of a Norwegian customer in March give UK 4, USA 2, Norway 1.
