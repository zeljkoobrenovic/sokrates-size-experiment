Purchasing can already see the open purchase orders of one supplier, and the overdue purchase orders overall and for one warehouse, but not the overdue purchase orders of one supplier, which is what they need before calling a supplier to chase late deliveries. Add that list: for a supplier and a date, the purchase orders of that supplier that have been sent but not received and whose expected date lies before that date, earliest expected date first, with their order lines. An order expected on the date itself is not yet overdue, and draft, received or cancelled orders never count. For example, on 13 July a supplier's sent orders expected on 8 July (two of them) and 12 July are overdue, listed in that order, while one expected on 20 July is not.
