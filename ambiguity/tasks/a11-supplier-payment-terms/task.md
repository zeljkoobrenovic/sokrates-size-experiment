# Suppliers get payment terms

Purchasing negotiates payment terms with every supplier: the number of days after the invoice date within which we have
to pay them. Today we cannot record this anywhere, so accounts payable keeps a separate spreadsheet that is always out
of date. Please let us enter payment terms (in days) when a supplier is registered and change them later when they are
renegotiated. A supplier for whom nothing was agreed has the standard terms of 30 days. The payment terms must be shown
with every supplier in the supplier listings: all suppliers by name, the suppliers of a country, the suppliers with at
least a given rating, and when a single supplier is looked up. For example, a Swedish supplier registered with 45 days
and later renegotiated to 14 days shows 14 days in all of these.
