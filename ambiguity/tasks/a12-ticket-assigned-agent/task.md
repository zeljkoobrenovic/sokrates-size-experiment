# Show which support agent handles a ticket

When a customer calls about a support ticket, nobody can tell who on the support team is handling it, so tickets get
picked up twice or not at all. When a ticket is logged we want to record the support agent it is assigned to (it may
also be left unassigned). The assigned agent must be shown with the ticket wherever tickets are listed: a customer's
open tickets, open tickets per priority, per region, per sales rep and per product, a customer's tickets about an
order, the old high-priority tickets of a region, the tickets open on a date, and when a ticket is looked up by its
number. Moving a ticket to in progress or closing it must not lose the agent. For example, ticket T-1 assigned to Sam
shows Sam in all of these lists, and an unassigned ticket shows no agent.
