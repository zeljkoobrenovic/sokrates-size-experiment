The landscape report's contributor lists are cut off at the `contributorsListLimit` setting of `_sokrates_landscape/config.json` (1000 by default). The tables of `contributors.html` and `contributors-recent.html` (the "All Contributors" and "Recently Active Contributors" pages embedded in the Contributors tab; the bots page and the teams pages are built the same way) stop after that many people and end with the note "The list is limited to N items (out of M).", and the bar chart of recent commits per contributor in the Contributors tab ("contributors sorted by recent commits") draws a bar only for the first N recently active contributors. On large landscapes this silently hides contributors.

Stop truncating: every contributor is exported to these tables and to the bar chart, regardless of the configured limit. A landscape with more contributors than `contributorsListLimit` lists all of them in `contributors.html` and `contributors-recent.html`, the "limited to" note is no longer shown, and the bar chart has one bar per recently active contributor. The ordering of the lists stays as it is (most recent commits first); the setting may remain in the configuration, it just no longer cuts these lists.
