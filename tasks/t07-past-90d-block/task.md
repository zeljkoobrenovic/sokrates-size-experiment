The Overview tab of the landscape report starts with an "active repositories" row of info blocks — all time, past 365d, past 180d, past 90d, past 30d — each showing the number of repositories updated in that period, with their main lines of code and the share of all main LOC underneath (e.g. "12 · past 90d · 1.2M LOC (35%)").

The "past 90d" block shows the wrong lines of code: it displays the LOC of the repositories updated in the past 180 days (the same figure as the neighbouring "past 180d" block), while its repository count and its percentage are the 90-day ones, so the block is internally inconsistent. Make the "past 90d" block show the main LOC of the repositories with commits in the past 90 days, like the other periods do. For example, with one repository of 500 LOC committed to last week and one of 2,000 LOC last committed to four months ago, the "past 180d" block says 2.5K LOC and the "past 90d" block 500 LOC.
